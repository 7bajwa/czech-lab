# Czech Lab: mock exams (unofficial, in the official A2 style)

## Format
- Exam: Zkouška z českého jazyka pro trvalý pobyt v ČR, level A2, new format valid from 11 April 2026 (the change affects only some Writing and Speaking tasks; level, certificate and dates stay the same).
- Order on exam day: Reading (40 min) and Writing (25 min) together without a break, 15 min break, Listening (about 35-40 min, as long as the recording), then the oral exam (Speaking, max. 15 min). The parts below are in the app's order, not the exam-day order.
- Total 110 points. Written block = Reading 25 + Writing 20 + Listening 25 = 70, pass = 42 (60 %). Speaking 40, pass = 24 (60 %). Failing either block means repeating the whole exam.
- Reading: 5 tasks, 25 items, 1 point each. Task 1 (5): match pictures 1-5 to short messages A-H (3 extra). Task 2 (5): one longer text, multiple choice A-D. Task 3 (4): match texts 11-14 to persons A-E (1 extra). Task 4 (6): short texts (ads, notices) with a gap, choose the word A-D. Task 5 (5): read a text and write one word or number into each gap.
- Writing: 2 tasks, 20 points. Task 1 Formulář (8): answer 3 questions in a form, full sentences, at least 10 words each. Task 2 E-mail (12): e-mail or letter (apology, invitation, greeting, request) with a correct opening and ending and a signature. Write at least 1 sentence for each of 5 picture prompts. At least 35 words, otherwise 0 points.
- Listening: 5 tasks x 5 items, 1 point each. Every recording plays twice. Task 1: 5 dialogues, choice A-D. Task 2: 5 short texts (announcements, radio, answering machine), choice A-D. Task 3: 5 texts, match to options A-G (2 extra). Task 4: 5 dialogues, match to pictures A-F (1 extra). Task 5: voice message, write one piece of information for each item. Short reading time before tasks (about 1 minute).
- Speaking: 4 tasks, no preparation time, examiner plus 2 raters. Task 1 (8): 8 questions on 2 everyday topics (4 + 4); you know the topics but do not see the questions. Task 2 (12): 2 role-play dialogues; you ask 4 questions per card from prompts (the last one is your own question). Task 3 (10): tell a story from 4 pictures in the past tense. Task 4 (7): agree with the examiner (a friend, informal "ty") on 1 of 3 pictured options and say why. Plus 3 points for pronunciation over the whole oral exam (37 + 3 = 40).
- Rating: the official handbook stresses task fulfilment, A2 everyday vocabulary, full sentences and pronunciation. The checklists below are this app's self-scoring approximation; the official raters' grid is not published in the handbook.
- In this app, pictures are replaced by short text descriptions, and the "0" example items are left out. Listening transcripts are read aloud by text-to-speech; play each one twice.
